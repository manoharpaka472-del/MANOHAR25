Passive Cooling Enclosure

Concept · In development

A compact enclosure that sheds heat without fans or power, using aluminium sheet and a finned surface.

Objective
Keep electronics cool silently, with zero energy use.
Working principle
Heat conducts through the aluminium walls to the fins, where natural convection carries it away.
Design focus
Fin spacing and orientation, sheet-metal bending and a clear air path.

Automatic Street Light

Automation project

A street light that switches on at dusk and off at dawn by sensing ambient light.

Objective
Remove manual switching and save energy by lighting only when dark.
Working principle
An LDR changes resistance with light; the control stage drives a relay that switches the lamp.
Design focus
Pole and bracket structure, plus a weather-protected housing for the electronics.

Soil Moisture Alarm

Sensing project

A probe-based alarm that warns when soil becomes too dry.

Objective
Prompt watering at the right time to protect plants.
Working principle
Two probes measure how well soil conducts; dry soil raises resistance and triggers the buzzer and LED.
Design focus
Probe geometry and placement, and sealing the electronics from water.
